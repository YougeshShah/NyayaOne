## 2. What We Collect

### Account Information
- Full name, email address, phone number (optional), password (stored

### Usage Data
- Course progress, mock test scores, MCQ answers, study time, bookmarks.
- Chat messages sent to the AI study assistant.
- Essay/writing submissions (for grading purposes).

### Payment Data
- Payment amount, course purchased, transaction status.
- We do NOT store your card/eSewa/Khalti login credentials — payments are
  processed directly by [eSewa/Khalti], and we only receive confirmation

### Technical Data
- IP address, browser/device type, login timestamps (for security and
  troubleshooting).

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- To provide and improve the platform (course access, progress tracking,
  test grading).
- To communicate with you (account notices, class reminders).
- To personalize your experience (recommended courses based on your
  stated interest).
- To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues.

## 4. Third-Party Services We Use
- **Payment processing**: eSewa, Khalti — they receive payment details
  necessary to process your transaction.
- **AI features** (study assistant chatbot, content generation): powered
  by Anthropic's Claude API. Messages you send to the chatbot are sent to
  Anthropic for processing. Do not share sensitive personal information
  in chat.
- **Video classes**: Jitsi Meet (no account required, no data sold).

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties.

## 5. Institution/Organization Data Sharing
If you are added to the platform by an institution (e.g. your IELTS
institute or law firm), that institution's staff can see:
- Your name, contact info, and enrollment status.
- Your progress and scores within courses they manage.

They cannot see your payment details, password, or activity in courses
unrelated to their institution.
